CWRU Faculty Tuition Reimbursement Advisor

Preliminary guidance • Application preparation • Reimbursement estimate

Before you begin
  • Only tuition charges are reimbursable. Exclude registration fees, books, laboratory fees, and other non-tuition expenses.
  • Maximum reimbursement is $2,500 per fiscal year (July 1–June 30) across all courses, subject to eligibility and other limits.
  • To receive reimbursement, you must earn C or higher, or Pass in a Pass/Fail course and submit official grade documentation.
  • Initial reimbursement requests require preauthorization. This advisor does not submit applications or grant approval. A course completed without submitting the application and obtaining preauthorization does not meet the normal reimbursement process.

Do not enter your name, employee ID, or other sensitive personal information.

Employment requirement: You must be employed at the start of the semester and remain employed through satisfactory completion of the course.
